# Code of Conduct

Ten98 projects adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as their code of conduct. It applies in every Ten98 repository, issue, pull request, discussion and other project space, and when someone represents the project in public.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and don't harass, insult or demean anyone.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **hello@ten98.io**. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
